Refund Policy - Acme SaaS Platform

Monthly plans: customers on monthly billing can request a full refund within 7 days
of the charge date. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within
5-10 business days.

Annual plans: customers on annual billing have a 30-day refund window from the date
of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled to
stop future renewals.

Enterprise plans: refunds for enterprise contracts are governed by the individual
service agreement signed with the customer, not this general policy. Contact your
account manager for contract-specific terms.

How to request a refund: email billing@acme.example with your account ID and the
reason for the request. Refund requests are typically reviewed within 2 business days.

Exceptions: refunds outside these windows may be granted at Acme's discretion in
cases of billing error, duplicate charges, or service outages exceeding 24 hours.

Data retention after cancellation: account data is retained for 90 days after
cancellation or refund, after which it is permanently deleted. Customers can request
an export of their data at any point during this 90-day window.
